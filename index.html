About Seed Cartographer

A free Minecraft seed map, structure finder and 3D world viewer

What it is

Seed Cartographer recreates a Minecraft world from its seed. Enter the seed, edition and version, and it maps the biomes and finds every village, stronghold, temple, monument, mansion, ancient city, trial chamber and more. Enter your own coordinates to find what is closest to you, then look at the land around you in 3D.

It uses the same world-generation rules as the game, so structure positions and biomes match your world. Everything runs in your browser: your seed is never sent anywhere.

Step by step

  1. Get your seed. In Java, type /seed in chat. In Bedrock, open the world's settings and copy the seed. Text seeds work too.
  2. Enter the seed. Paste it into the Seed box (on a phone, open the World tab).
  3. Choose edition and version. Pick Java or Bedrock and the version the world was created in. Tick Large Biomes if your world uses it.
  4. Press Load world. The map draws and the structure list fills in within a few seconds.
  5. Explore the map. Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom, and tap a structure icon to see its coordinates and copy a teleport command.
  6. Add your location. Press F3 in Java (or turn on Show Coordinates in Bedrock), enter X Y Z, then press Show on map. The nearest structures are listed with distance and direction.
  7. Open the 3D view. Press View in 3D to see the terrain around you or around a structure. Press Walk to explore on foot.
  8. Switch dimension. Choose Nether or End to find fortresses, bastions and end cities. Nether coordinates are worked out from your Overworld position.

How accurate is it?

Structure positions and biomes are exact for Java worlds. Ground height in the 3D view follows the world's terrain noise but is smoothed, so cliffs and peaks are softer and there are no caves; trees and flowers are placed for decoration. Newer additions such as 26.2's Sulfur Caves and 26.3's Dappled Forest and Abandoned Camps are not drawn yet.

Questions

Why isn't a structure there when I go to it?

Check that the edition, version and Large Biomes setting match your world. Structures can also fail to generate in the game if the terrain is unsuitable, and the coordinates point to the corner of the structure's starting chunk.

Does it work for Bedrock?

Biomes match Java from Bedrock 1.18 onward. Bedrock places many structures differently from Java, so treat Bedrock structure positions as a guide and check them in-game.

Is my seed uploaded?

No. The world generator runs inside your browser, and your seed and location are only saved on your own device.

How do Nether coordinates work?

One block in the Nether equals eight in the Overworld. When you switch to the Nether, your Overworld X and Z are divided by 8 for you.
